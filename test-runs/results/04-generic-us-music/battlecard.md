# ⚔️ Spotify vs YouTube Music

App Review Battlecard · Google Play US · 2026-11-22

> **Spotify's 26.6% negative share beats YouTube Music's 40.4%, but Spotify's own ad and crash complaints must be fixed before it can lead on rival weaknesses.**

## 🟢 Opportunities (where rivals are weaker)

- **Refunds and renewals that just work**: 6% of YouTube Music's 1–2★ reviews are about payments & refunds, vs 2% of yours. (confidence: medium)
  - Message: Billing should never be the reason you cancel. Clear renewals and fast help when something goes wrong, wherever you are.
  - “Can't even play one song or make my own que it's like I'm on free mode or something I'm canceling my subscription”
  - “Yall need to fix this stupid reactivation error where it's not letting you renew based on your location”
- **YouTube Music users are angrier about product, food, service or content quality**: 11% of YouTube Music's 1–2★ reviews are about product, food, service or content quality, vs 8% of yours. (confidence: low)
  - Message: Lead with your strength in product, food, service or content quality in campaigns aimed at YouTube Music users.
  - “Insanely clunky. Making custom music playlists is tedious, and the algorithm is very bad at picking songs. It's frustrating when these playlists appear on my channel.”
  - “Home page is giving me irrelevant recommendations since yesterday.”

## 🔴 Threats (where you are weaker)

- **Free trials that feel like traps**: 12% of your 1–2★ reviews are about prices, fees & subscriptions, vs 3% of YouTube Music's. (confidence: high)
  - Action: Pricing & Growth should make trial terms and charges explicit at signup and send a reminder before the first charge.
