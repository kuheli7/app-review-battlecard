MethodThe most recent Google Play reviews per app. An AI model (anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5) labels every 1–2★ review with one theme; shares and gaps are computed in code. We compare the mix of complaints, not average stars, because in-app rating prompts inflate star averages. Each gap carries a confidence from a two-proportion z-test (high: about 95% sure it is not chance). Reviews labelled: Swiggy 100 of 100 (21 vague), Zomato 100 of 100 (20 vague). A second, independent AI pass re-labelled 30 of Swiggy's reviews and agreed on 83.3%. Quotes are real reviews; reviewer names are never stored. Made with App Review Battlecard on Apify.
