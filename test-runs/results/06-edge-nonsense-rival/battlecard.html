MethodThe most recent Google Play reviews per app. An AI model (anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5) labels every 1–2★ review with one theme; shares and gaps are computed in code. We compare the mix of complaints, not average stars, because in-app rating prompts inflate star averages. Each gap carries a confidence from a two-proportion z-test (high: about 95% sure it is not chance). Skipped: "qwzxkjhgfdsa" (not found on Google Play). Reviews labelled: Zepto 100 of 100 (8 vague), Blinkit 74 of 74 (13 vague). A second, independent AI pass re-labelled 30 of Zepto's reviews and agreed on 90%. Quotes are real reviews; reviewer names are never stored. Made with App Review Battlecard on Apify.
