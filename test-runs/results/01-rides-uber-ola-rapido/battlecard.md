# ⚔️ Uber vs Ola vs Rapido

App Review Battlecard · Google Play IN · 2026-11-22

> **Ola's 1–2★ reviews are 57.6% of its total, versus Uber's 24.8%, so Ola's trust problems are the clearest opening for Uber to win switchers.**

## 🟢 Opportunities (where rivals are weaker)

- **Real people answer when a ride goes wrong**: 12% of Ola's 1–2★ reviews are about customer support, vs 5% of yours. (confidence: medium)
  - Message: When something goes wrong on your trip, you deserve a real response. Get help fast, with support that follows through.
  - “very poor customer service....had to pay for wrong ride automatically booked by ola ....even after raising a concern ..no proper response..very poor support”
  - “worst cab booking app. no customer support system available. No one should use this app ever. worst experience with driver in last trip but no support system available”
- **Rapido users are angrier about account, login & security**: 7% of Rapido's 1–2★ reviews are about account, login & security, vs 2% of yours. (confidence: medium)
  - Message: Lead with your strength in account, login & security in campaigns aimed at Rapido users.
  - “why same otps for all rides its not safe for customers. rapido is really rapid you have no time to change otps for next rides”
  - “worst app profile doesn't get verified for bike driver in Pune City even after uploading documents since 2 weeks.”
- **Rides that actually get accepted and arrive on time**: 30% of Rapido's 1–2★ reviews are about delivery & fulfilment (late, cancelled, missing, no-show), vs 21% of yours. (confidence: medium)
  - Fix before you advertise this: "Driver cancels or demands extra cash after accepting" is on your own fix-first list (mentioned in 23 of your 100 recent 1–2★ reviews).
  - Message: Tired of drivers skipping your ride? With Uber, we match you quickly and keep you informed until you're picked up.
  - “Worst app ever.. Drivers never takes ride. If don't want to work then shut down the business, why customers to waste their time ..”
  - “These people hardly accept any rides these days. They prefer delivering parcels instead,so disappointed. Deleting this app right away”

## 🔴 Threats (where you are weaker)

- **Rival fare surprises are pulling price-sensitive riders**: 31% of your 1–2★ reviews are about prices, fees & subscriptions, vs 19% of Ola's. (confidence: medium)
  - Action: Pricing & Growth: publish a fare-transparency promise and show full price upfront to counter rival fare-mismatch complaints.
  - “sabse bekar app hai kiraya dikhata kuchh aur hai leta kuchh aur hai isse achcha green sm hai uber is very bed app ☹️🫤”
  - “Worst experience, drivers do not accept rides at night in Railway station, instead they charge triple amount as a private taxi”
- **Charge and refund complaints are spreading to rivals' users**: 10% of your 1–2★ reviews are about payments & refunds, vs 7% of Rapido's. (confidence: low)
  - Action: Payments & Support: audit cancellation-fee logic and make refunds visible in-app to keep users from switching over billing confusion.
  - “not only are they terrible at actually finding you a driver, they will STILL charge you for a ride you cancelled.”
  - “I always get charges I don't know about. maybe it's a way of extorting my account but y'all can do without me.”
- **Rider experience complaints around driver conduct and contact**: 14% of your 1–2★ reviews are about product, food, service or content quality, vs 11% of Rapido's. (confidence: low)
  - Action: Operations & Partners: set a driver-conduct standard and contact-policy review so Uber doesn't inherit similar rider-experience churn.
  - “some one answering the call. after booking and call the driver. avoid this ASAP. good people struggle for that issue”
  - “asks to be rated= 0 starts. instead of using text it sends a WhatsApp. this is what i consider exceptionally rude.”
