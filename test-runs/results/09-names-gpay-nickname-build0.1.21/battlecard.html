MethodThe most recent Google Play reviews per app. An AI model (anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5) labels every 1–2★ review with one theme; shares and gaps are computed in code. We compare the mix of complaints, not average stars, because in-app rating prompts inflate star averages. Each gap carries a confidence from a two-proportion z-test (high: about 95% sure it is not chance). Reviews labelled: Google Pay 46 of 46 (27 vague), PhonePe 49 of 49 (27 vague), Paytm 38 of 38 (17 vague). A second, independent AI pass re-labelled 23 of Google Pay's reviews and agreed on 95.7%.
